Bathroom Remodel Cost in Everett: Compare a Complete Scope

By NW General Contractor. Updated October 11, 2026 after a substantive scope and source review.

What determines your bathroom price?

A fixture refresh, shower rebuild and full layout change are different projects. Room area alone cannot price them. We do not have a verified local project dataset supporting a typical Everett bathroom price. Use this checklist to request a measured bathroom remodeling estimate and compare proposals on the same basis.

Choose the work you actually need

  • Refresh: identify which sound surfaces and fixtures remain and which finish items change.
  • Replace in place: specify the vanity, toilet, shower or tub, flooring, lighting and connections.
  • Reconfigure: assess wall changes, fixture relocation, utility routes and affected adjoining rooms.
  • Improve access: discuss doorway clearance, usable space, shower entry, backing for supports and the needs of the people using the room. A product described as accessible does not by itself establish compliance with a particular standard.

Look beyond the visible tile

Ask the proposal to identify the shower or tub assembly, substrate, waterproofing system, transitions, penetrations and installation requirements. Decorative tile is only one part of the assembly. Agree on any inspection or testing steps before work is covered. Coordinate ventilation, electrical work and plumbing with the relevant qualified trades.

Use a budget worksheet instead of a headline range

  • Identify dimensions, products, installation, removal and disposal separately.
  • Include protection, access, design, permit responsibility, tax and restoration.
  • Label allowances, exclusions and owner-supplied products.
  • Agree how concealed conditions are documented, priced and authorized.
  • Keep owner contingency separate from the contract price; select it for the actual uncertainty rather than a universal percentage.

For each item, record quantity, supply-only or installed basis, selected product and who purchases it. Add a separate line for glass, trim, accessories and painting if not included. Avoid counting installation twice when a supplier provides a complete installed price. Compare the same selections before deciding that one proposal is cheaper.

Assess hidden conditions before promising savings

Existing water damage, a deteriorated subfloor or unsuitable backing can expand the work. Ask what can be inspected before pricing and what will remain unknown until demolition. Keeping the layout may avoid some relocation work, but it does not guarantee that existing connections can remain unchanged.

Before disturbing painted surfaces in pre-1978 housing, review EPA lead-safe renovation guidance and discuss assessment and qualified help. Identify other suspect materials before demolition. A house’s age alone does not establish the condition or material of its plumbing, wiring or finishes.

Permits and time without the bathroom

Confirm the parcel jurisdiction before requesting permit advice. An Everett postal address does not necessarily mean the property is inside city limits. Use Everett Permit Services for city property or Snohomish County residential permitting for unincorporated property. Describe the exact work, including structural and trade changes. Ask which approvals, plans, fees and inspections apply; an exemption from a building permit does not remove other applicable requirements.

Separate decisions and ordering from construction. Ask when the room becomes unavailable, how other facilities remain usable, and which inspections, product delivery, curing or drying requirements control the next stage. A universal two-week or four-week promise cannot account for every project.

Budget for use, then consider resale

Prioritize required repairs, useful storage and the way your household uses the room. If selling is the main objective, review actual comparable homes with a local real-estate professional. A published cost-recovery percentage is not a valuation of your home or a guarantee that remodeling will increase the sale price.

Questions before you start

Can a cosmetic bathroom update avoid permits?

Describe each change to the correct authority. Cosmetic finish work and plumbing or electrical changes are not interchangeable scopes; confirm applicable approvals before work.

How do I compare a refresh with a full remodel?

Request separate written scopes showing retained items, replacements and unresolved conditions. Compare the function, repairs and disruption as well as the total.
