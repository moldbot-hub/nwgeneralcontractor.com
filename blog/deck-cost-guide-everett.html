Deck and Patio Cost in Everett: Build a Comparable Estimate

By NW General Contractor. Updated October 11, 2026 after a substantive scope and source review.

Price the structure and the site together

A ground-level patio and an elevated deck solve different site problems. A useful estimate identifies the intended area, height, access and supporting construction. We do not present unsupported price ranges as Everett project data. Start with the same layout and written scope when comparing options.

Separate four common project types

  • Surface maintenance or repair: identify what remains and the evidence that the retained structure is suitable.
  • Deck resurfacing: assess framing, connections, stairs and supports before ordering new boards.
  • New or replacement deck: scope footings, framing, house connection, decking, stairs, guards and drainage.
  • Patio: scope excavation, base, surface, edges, grade, runoff and transitions to doors or paths.

What belongs in a complete proposal?

  • Identify dimensions, products, installation, removal and disposal separately.
  • Include protection, access, design, permit responsibility, tax and restoration.
  • Label allowances, exclusions and owner-supplied products.
  • Agree how concealed conditions are documented, priced and authorized.
  • Keep owner contingency separate from the contract price; select it for the actual uncertainty rather than a universal percentage.
  • Site measurement, drawings and any engineering needed for the design.
  • Demolition, disposal, excavation and access for equipment or hand-carrying materials.
  • Named decking or paving product and the full supporting assembly.
  • Stairs, guards, handrails, house interfaces and weather-management details.
  • Related electrical work, covers, utility conflicts or retaining work, separately identified.

Avoid misleading square-foot comparisons

Confirm whether the quoted area is the finished walking surface and whether stairs or multiple levels are included. Two decks with the same area may have different heights, supports and access. A board-only price omits the structure and installation. Ask for a total installed scope, then use unit rates only to understand specific quantities.

Plan materials and maintenance together

Compare product packages using the same layout. The deck material guide covers wood and composite selection questions. Ask for cleaning, finish and warranty instructions for the actual product. A decking warranty is not a guarantee for the whole structure or a prediction of maintenance spending.

Permits and sequence

Confirm the parcel jurisdiction before requesting permit advice. An Everett postal address does not necessarily mean the property is inside city limits. Use Everett Permit Services for city property or Snohomish County residential permitting for unincorporated property. Describe the exact work, including structural and trade changes. Ask which approvals, plans, fees and inspections apply; an exemption from a building permit does not remove other applicable requirements.

Do not decide exemption from area or height alone: attachment, use, location and other work can matter. Ask the authority for the applicable checklist. Agree on approval, material delivery, site preparation, inspection and weather dependencies before setting a start date. Patio curing or deck finish requirements should follow the chosen system.

Reduce optional work before structural work

Ask for a simpler layout and separately priced decorative options. A future cover or heavy feature may need to be considered in the original design even if installed later. Do not substitute cheaper supports, connections or drainage details merely to reach a headline budget.

Prepare for an estimate visit

Bring dimensions, safe ground-level photos, property records and the intended furniture or use. Identify stairs, slopes, drainage concerns and equipment access. Discuss deck building or patio and concrete work with the address and budget boundary.
