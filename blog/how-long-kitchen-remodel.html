How Long Does a Kitchen Remodel Take? Plan the Dependencies

By NW General Contractor. Updated October 11, 2026 after a substantive scope and source review.

A calendar needs a defined scope

A kitchen refresh and a structural reconfiguration do not follow the same schedule. We do not promise a standard number of weeks before checking the work, product availability and approval requirements. Ask for milestone dates and the assumptions behind them.

Before demolition

  • Measure the room and resolve layout and structural questions.
  • Finalize cabinet dimensions, appliance specifications and installation clearances.
  • Confirm product ordering responsibility and verified delivery expectations.
  • Complete applicable design and permit steps.
  • Plan temporary cooking, utility interruptions, dust separation and safe access.

During construction

Ask how protection, removal, investigation, rough trade work, inspections, cabinets, surfaces, fixtures and finishes are sequenced. Some measurements, such as a countertop template, depend on earlier work being installed. Do not schedule every trade as if all dependencies can happen in parallel.

If the plan changes

A late selection, damaged delivery, concealed condition or inspection correction can affect later work. Ask who updates the schedule, how changed scope is approved and when revised dates will be communicated. Distinguish a target from a confirmed appointment.

Ask about the days that affect your household

  • When are sink, range and refrigerator access interrupted?
  • Which days require the home to be vacant or particular rooms closed?
  • When will major deliveries arrive and where will they be stored?
  • What inspections must occur before walls or other work are covered?
  • What remains at walkthrough and how is closeout documented?

Link the schedule to the budget

Temporary accommodation, storage and repeated setup can affect the total. Compare the kitchen budget checklist with the schedule and bring both to your kitchen remodeling consultation.

Start with the parcel jurisdiction. An Everett mailing address does not establish that the property is inside city limits. Use Everett Permit Services for city property and county residential permit guidance for unincorporated Snohomish County. Ask which building, plumbing, mechanical, electrical or site-work approvals apply to the actual scope. A building-permit exemption does not waive other applicable requirements.
