Kitchen Remodeling in Everett, WA

Kitchen remodeling in Everett and Snohomish County. Plan layout, cabinets, counters, permits and a clear written scope with NW General Contractor.

Plan a kitchen that works for your household

NW General Contractor handles kitchen remodeling inquiries in Everett and Snohomish County. Start with the problems you need to solve: insufficient storage, difficult circulation, worn finishes or a layout that no longer works. We review the location and scope before proposing the next step.

Choose what stays and what changes

  • Cabinets and storage: identify dimensions, door and drawer access, retained cabinets and replacement runs.
  • Counters and backsplash: coordinate edges, seams, sink openings, outlets and transitions.
  • Appliances: confirm actual models, clearances, utility requirements and who orders them.
  • Flooring and trim: specify preparation, adjacent-room transitions and finishes to retain.
  • Layout and building systems: discuss wall changes, lighting, plumbing and any structural design.

From site visit to written scope

Bring photos, appliance information, available plans and a list of priorities. The proposal should distinguish fixed work, allowances, owner-supplied items and exclusions. Agree how concealed conditions and selection changes will be priced and authorized before construction.

Use the kitchen cost and estimate guide to compare proposals on the same basis. No generic price range or resale percentage can establish the right budget for your home.

Permits and household disruption

Start with the parcel jurisdiction. An Everett mailing address does not establish that the property is inside city limits. Use Everett Permit Services for city property and county residential permit guidance for unincorporated Snohomish County. Ask which building, plumbing, mechanical, electrical or site-work approvals apply to the actual scope. A building-permit exemption does not waive other applicable requirements.

Plan the time without normal cooking facilities, dust separation, deliveries and inspections. See the kitchen schedule checklist before agreeing on dates.

Before demolition in older homes, identify materials that need assessment and the appropriate qualified trades. For pre-1978 housing, review EPA lead-safe renovation guidance before disturbing painted surfaces. Do not treat do-it-yourself demolition as an automatic saving; protection, testing, disposal and repair of unintended damage belong in the decision.

Coordinate adjoining rooms

A kitchen project can affect nearby flooring, walls and trim. If several rooms or building systems need work, compare a coordinated whole-home renovation scope instead of separate estimates that leave gaps.

Questions before you start

Can I keep my existing layout?

Yes, retaining a workable layout is an option to assess. Confirm the condition of retained items and the connections required by new products.

Will a remodel pay for itself at resale?

That cannot be guaranteed. Budget for your needs and ask a local real-estate professional about comparable properties if resale is part of your decision.

What does a free estimate cover?

Discuss the initial review and what information is needed. Confirm separately priced investigation, design or permit work before authorizing it.
